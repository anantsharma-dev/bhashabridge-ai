# BhashaBridge AI Development Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap documents the complete development journey of BhashaBridge AI.

Each sprint represents one functional milestone. A sprint is considered complete only when the interface, reusable components, responsiveness, accessibility, and production build are verified.

The roadmap also serves as a historical record of development progress.

---

## Current Phase — Foundation

Sprint 1 established the visual foundation of BhashaBridge AI.

The dashboard was redesigned into a child-friendly educational home screen inspired by Duolingo ABC, Khan Academy Kids, and Material Design 3 while preserving the existing application architecture.

---

## Phase 2 — Interactive Learning Modules

Sprint 2 introduces Voice Translation.

Sprint 3 builds Flashcards Explorer.

Sprint 4 creates the Worksheet Generator.

Sprint 5 develops the Lesson Planner.

Sprint 6 implements Stories & Read Along.

Sprint 7 builds the Offline Library.

Sprint 8 creates Teacher Profile and Classroom Dashboard.

Together these sprints transform the application into a complete multilingual educational platform.

---

## Phase 3 — Artificial Intelligence Layer

Sprint 9 integrates Whisper speech recognition, Gemini translation services, multilingual lesson generation, worksheet generation, OCR architecture, and speech synthesis.

This sprint introduces the application's AI infrastructure without changing educational modules.

---

## Phase 4 — Offline Curriculum Platform

Sprint 10 introduces curriculum synchronization, downloadable learning packs, offline-first storage, version management, classroom synchronization, and Android tablet deployment preparation.

This prepares the application for real-world classroom usage.

---

## Future Milestones

The roadmap extends beyond Sprint 10.

Future milestones include adaptive learning recommendations, handwriting recognition, pronunciation scoring, classroom analytics, multilingual assessments, offline AI inference, parent engagement, and deployment through Android tablets across MTB-MLE schools.

These milestones remain outside the MVP and will be developed after the first working educational prototype.
