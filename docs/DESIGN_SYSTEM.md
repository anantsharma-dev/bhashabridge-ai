# BhashaBridge AI Design System

## Purpose

The BhashaBridge AI Design System defines the visual identity, interaction language, accessibility standards, mascot usage, typography hierarchy, spacing system, color palette, illustrations, icons, animations, and reusable component specifications used across the application.

This document ensures visual consistency across every screen and sprint.

---

## Design Philosophy

BhashaBridge AI is designed for children aged six to eleven studying in multilingual classrooms.

The interface prioritizes clarity, curiosity, warmth, trust, and accessibility over visual spectacle.

The design language combines the friendliness of Duolingo ABC, the educational calmness of Khan Academy Kids, the simplicity of Google Read Along, and Google's Material Design 3 component philosophy.

Every visual decision should reduce cognitive load while encouraging exploration.

---

## Brand Personality

BhashaBridge AI should feel like a cheerful classroom companion instead of a technology product.

The application should communicate encouragement rather than achievement pressure.

The interface celebrates curiosity, effort, and multilingual identity.

---

## Color System

The primary background color is warm cream (`#FFFDF7`).

Primary blue communicates learning, trust, and interaction.

Primary green represents success and progress.

Primary orange represents curiosity, activities, and playful interaction.

Purple represents AI-assisted learning.

Coral represents celebrations and badges.

Yellow represents rewards and stars.

Soft pastel versions of every primary color are used for educational cards and backgrounds.

Dark backgrounds should never become the primary interface theme.

---

## Typography

Poppins is the default interface font for navigation, buttons, labels, and interface text.

Baloo 2 is reserved for headings aimed at children, lesson titles, celebrations, and playful educational moments.

Noto Sans Devanagari renders Hindi content.

Noto Sans Ol Chiki renders Santali content written in Ol Chiki.

Typography should remain highly legible on 10-inch Android tablets.

---

## Mascot System

Johar Hornbill is the official mascot of BhashaBridge AI.

The mascot represents encouragement, curiosity, multilingual friendship, and local ecological identity.

Johar appears in hero sections, celebrations, empty states, speech activities, reading achievements, and onboarding experiences.

The mascot should never become decorative clutter or occupy instructional space.

---

## Illustration Style

Illustrations follow flat colorful educational artwork with rounded shapes, expressive facial expressions, and soft gradients.

Illustrations should represent diverse Indian children, tribal communities, forests, animals, classrooms, festivals, and everyday village life respectfully.

---

## Component Language

Cards follow Material Design 3 with 24px corner radius.

Buttons use rounded pill or rounded rectangle shapes with large touch targets.

Inputs are oversized for tablet interaction.

Chips represent languages, grades, categories, and learning filters.

Progress bars use friendly rounded shapes.

Badges communicate milestones rather than competition.

---

## Motion Principles

Animations are subtle and meaningful.

Cards lift slightly on interaction.

Buttons compress gently during touch.

Stars celebrate achievements.

Confetti appears only for important milestones.

Motion should reinforce learning without distracting children.

---

## Accessibility Standards

The interface follows WCAG-inspired accessibility practices adapted for primary education.

High contrast, readable typography, large spacing, touch targets larger than 48dp, multilingual rendering, keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and reduced motion support are expected across the application.

---

## Responsive Grid

The primary layout targets Android tablets in landscape orientation.

The design scales gracefully to portrait tablets and mobile devices while preserving educational hierarchy.

Tablet-first responsiveness is mandatory for every reusable component.

---

## Design Governance

Every new component added to the project must follow this design system before being merged into the application.

This document functions as the single source of truth for visual consistency.
