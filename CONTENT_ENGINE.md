# BhashaBridge Content Engine

## Purpose

The Content Engine defines how educational content is created, structured, translated, narrated, and personalized inside BhashaBridge AI.

Every story, flashcard, worksheet, quiz, vocabulary card, lesson plan, and classroom activity follows this document.

The goal is educational consistency across languages, grades, and subjects.

---

## Content Philosophy

Content should encourage curiosity, observation, reasoning, empathy, creativity, and multilingual literacy.

Lessons should begin with familiar experiences before introducing unfamiliar concepts.

Examples should reflect children's everyday environments while expanding toward wider Indian and global knowledge.

---

## Flashcards

Every flashcard teaches one concept.

Each card includes an illustration, multilingual vocabulary, pronunciation, audio, example sentence, fun fact, and interactive quiz.

Flashcards remain grouped by grade, subject, language, and difficulty.

---

## Stories

Stories combine local folklore, NCERT reading material, tribal oral traditions, Indian literature, and world children's stories.

Each story contains multilingual narration, vocabulary support, comprehension questions, pronunciation support, and teacher discussion prompts.

Stories remain age-appropriate and culturally respectful.

---

## Worksheets

Worksheets align with NCERT and NIPUN Bharat learning outcomes.

Every worksheet specifies grade, subject, competency, language, activity type, printable layout, answer key, and classroom objective.

Generated worksheets remain editable by teachers.

---

## Lesson Plans

Lesson plans include learning objectives, multilingual vocabulary, classroom activities, storytelling prompts, assessments, and extension activities.

Teachers can generate bilingual lesson plans customized for classroom language combinations.

---

## Quizzes

Quizzes assess understanding through games rather than examinations.

Activities include matching, listening, picture identification, sequencing, pronunciation, memory games, and storytelling comprehension.

Difficulty adapts to grade level.

---

## Translation Rules

Translations preserve meaning rather than literal word substitution.

Language services normalize mixed-language input before translation.

Educational vocabulary follows grade-appropriate terminology.

---

## Subject Expansion

The content engine supports foundational literacy, mathematics, environmental studies, history, geography, civics, psychology, philosophy, ecology, arts, music, and cultural knowledge.

Each subject contains vocabulary, stories, activities, and assessments linked to curriculum competencies.

---

## Child-Centered Writing Style

Sentences remain short, concrete, encouraging, and conversational.

Complex concepts are introduced through stories, questions, examples, and illustrations.

Every lesson includes opportunities for children to observe, speak, listen, read, draw, and ask questions.

---

## Cultural Representation

Content includes local Jharkhand contexts alongside broader Indian and global perspectives.

Representation should include tribal communities, diverse Indian cultures, different family structures, multiple ecosystems, festivals, historical figures, artists, scientists, and everyday classroom experiences without stereotyping communities.

---

## AI Content Governance

AI-generated content must remain curriculum-aligned, age-appropriate, multilingual, and factually grounded.

When AI generates stories, worksheets, or lesson plans, outputs must reference curriculum competencies and preserve multilingual accessibility across Santali, Hindi, and English.
